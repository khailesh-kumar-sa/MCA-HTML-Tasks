Royal Challengers Bengaluru

A journey through RCB's achievements, legendary players, and unforgettable moments in IPL history.

About RCB

Royal Challengers Bengaluru, commonly known as RCB, represents the city of Bengaluru in the Indian Premier League. The franchise is famous for its passionate supporters and some of the biggest names in cricket.

Over the years, RCB has created countless memorable moments and developed one of the strongest fan cultures in the IPL.

RCB Achievements

IPL 2025 Champions

RCB won its first IPL championship in 2025, marking one of the most memorable moments in the franchise's history.

IPL Final Appearances

Before winning the title, RCB reached the IPL final in 2009, 2011, and 2016.

Individual Records

RCB has been home to several record-breaking batting performances, including outstanding seasons from some of the greatest players in IPL history.

Famous RCB Players

Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is arguably the most iconic RCB player. He has represented the franchise throughout his IPL career and has produced numerous memorable performances.

AB de Villiers

Known as Mr. 360, AB de Villiers entertained RCB fans with his innovative batting, spectacular catches, and match-winning performances.

Chris Gayle

Chris Gayle was one of the most destructive batsmen to play for RCB. His enormous sixes and explosive innings made him a fan favorite.

Rahul Dravid

Rahul Dravid was part of RCB's early IPL history and is widely regarded as one of India's greatest batsmen.

RCB Fan Culture

RCB has one of the most passionate fan bases in cricket. The famous "Ee Sala Cup Namde" spirit represents the optimism and loyalty of RCB supporters.
