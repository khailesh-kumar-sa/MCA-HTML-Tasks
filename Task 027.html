Ancient Egypt

CSS provides several properties for controlling how text appears on a webpage. Three important properties are font-size, font-weight, and font-style.

1. font-size

The pyramids of Egypt are among the most famous structures from the ancient world.

The font-size property controls the size of text. In the paragraph above, the text has been made larger using font-size: 28px;.

2. font-weight

Ancient Egyptian civilization developed along the Nile River.

The font-weight property controls how thick or bold the characters appear. The paragraph above uses font-weight: bold;.

3. font-style

Egyptian hieroglyphs were used as a writing system in ancient Egypt.

The font-style property controls whether text appears normal, italic, or oblique. The paragraph above uses font-style: italic;.
