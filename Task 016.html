The Wizarding World

Welcome to the magical world of Hogwarts, where young witches and wizards learn spells, potions, magical creatures, and the history of the wizarding world.

Hogwarts School

Hogwarts is a magical school where students are sorted into four houses: Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw, and Slytherin.

Magical Subjects

Students learn subjects such as Defence Against the Dark Arts, Potions, Transfiguration, Charms, and Herbology.

Advanced students can explore more specialized areas of magic as they progress through their years.

Quidditch

Quidditch is a popular wizarding sport played on flying broomsticks. Players compete to score points and catch the Golden Snitch.
