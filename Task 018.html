Cricket World

Cricket is one of the most popular sports in the world, played between two teams with eleven players on each side.

About Cricket

Cricket is played in different formats including Test matches, One Day Internationals, and Twenty20 matches. Each format provides a different style of competition.

Batting

Batters attempt to score runs by hitting the ball and running between the wickets or hitting boundaries. Good timing and technique are important skills for a successful batter.

Bowling

Bowlers try to dismiss batters while limiting the number of runs scored. Fast bowling, spin bowling, swing, and variations make bowling an important part of the game.

Fielding

Fielders help prevent runs and create opportunities for wickets through catches, run-outs, and accurate throws.
