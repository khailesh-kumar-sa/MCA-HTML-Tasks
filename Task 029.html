Underwater Exploration

The ocean is one of the least explored environments on Earth. This webpage demonstrates three CSS text properties using an underwater exploration theme.

1. text-transform

The text-transform property changes the capitalization of text without changing the original HTML content.

Discover the hidden world beneath the ocean.

text-transform: uppercase;

DISCOVER THE HIDDEN WORLD BENEATH THE OCEAN.

text-transform: lowercase;

explore coral reefs and discover marine life.

text-transform: capitalize;

2. text-decoration

The text-decoration property adds visual decorations such as underlines, overlines, and lines through text.

Underwater Research

text-decoration: underline;

Deep Sea Exploration

text-decoration: overline;

Unknown Ocean Territory

text-decoration: line-through;

3. letter-spacing

The letter-spacing property controls the amount of horizontal space between individual characters.

OCEAN

letter-spacing: 8px;

Deep Sea

letter-spacing: normal;

Marine Biology

letter-spacing: -1px;
