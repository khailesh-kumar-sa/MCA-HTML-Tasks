Friends and Their Qualities

Harry Potter
Brave, determined, loyal, and willing to stand up for others even when facing difficult situations.
Ron Weasley
Loyal, humorous, supportive, and courageous. He always stands beside his closest friends.
Hermione Granger
Intelligent, hardworking, responsible, and resourceful. She often uses her knowledge to help her friends.
Ginny Weasley
Confident, independent, courageous, and determined. She is also a talented Quidditch player.
Draco Malfoy
Ambitious, competitive, intelligent, and resourceful. His character also shows complexity and inner conflict.
Weasley Twins
Fred and George are creative, mischievous, adventurous, humorous, and excellent at inventing magical products.
Cedric Diggory
Kind, fair, talented, respectful, and courageous. He treats others with dignity even while competing.
Luna Lovegood
Imaginative, unique, honest, open-minded, and remarkably loyal to her friends.
Sirius Black
Brave, fiercely loyal, protective, rebellious, and deeply devoted to the people he considers family.
Cho Chang
Talented, kind, emotional, thoughtful, and courageous. She is also an accomplished Quidditch player.
Neville Longbottom
Kind, humble, loyal, and courageous. His journey shows tremendous personal growth and confidence.
Colin Creevey
Enthusiastic, curious, energetic, and fascinated by magic and the people around him.
