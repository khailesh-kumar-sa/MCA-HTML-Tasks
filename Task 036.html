My Success Story

Success is not always about achieving one huge goal. For me, it is about learning from every experience, turning ideas into reality, and continuing to move forward even when the journey becomes difficult.

My Academic Journey

My journey in technology began with my Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA). During this period, I developed an interest in programming, web development, cybersecurity, and software projects. Completing my BCA became an important milestone in my academic journey.

After completing my BCA, I started my Master of Computer Applications (MCA). This marks the beginning of another chapter where I can deepen my technical knowledge and work towards bigger goals in the technology field.

White Devils Tech

One of the most meaningful parts of my journey has been building White Devils Tech. What started as an idea around technology education gradually developed into something much bigger — a platform focused on learning, collaboration, projects, and technology.

White Devils Tech represents my interest in combining technology with entrepreneurship. I have worked on ideas involving cybersecurity, software development, technical education, and projects that can help students gain practical experience.

The vision behind White Devils Tech is not just to teach technology but to create an environment where people can learn, collaborate, build projects, and develop skills that are useful beyond the classroom.

Learning Through Projects

Projects have played an important role in my development. Working on real applications has taught me that understanding a concept and actually implementing it are two very different things.

From web development to cybersecurity projects, every project has introduced a new problem to solve and another opportunity to improve my technical skills.

Every mistake became a lesson, every project became experience, and every new challenge became another opportunity to improve.

More Than Academics

My journey has also been shaped by creativity and curiosity. I enjoy exploring ideas beyond regular academic work, whether it is designing webpages, creating digital content, researching history, experimenting with technology, or developing new project ideas.

The Next Chapter

Completing my BCA and beginning my MCA is not the end of my journey. It is the transition into another chapter. At the same time, White Devils Tech continues to represent an entrepreneurial side of my journey and a long-term vision that I want to develop further.

There are still many things I want to learn, many projects I want to build, and many ideas I want to turn into reality. My success story is therefore not a finished story. It is still being written.

My Vision

I want to build a future where my education, technical skills, creativity, and entrepreneurial mindset come together. My goal is to keep learning, create meaningful work, and build something that has value for other people as well as myself.
