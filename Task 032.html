About Me

If my life were a book, it would be a story that is still being written. Every chapter would represent a different phase of my journey, with new experiences, lessons, ambitions, and people shaping the pages along the way.

One of the important chapters so far has been my journey through computer applications. I completed my Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA), and I have now started my Master of Computer Applications (MCA) to continue developing my knowledge and skills in technology.

Technology is a major part of my story. I enjoy exploring areas such as programming, web development, cybersecurity, and creating software projects. I like learning by building things rather than simply reading about them.

Another chapter of my book would be about creativity. From designing webpages and graphics to exploring ideas for projects and content, I enjoy turning ideas into something tangible.

My story is not limited to academics or technology. History, Tamil culture, books, music, cricket, and many other interests have their own pages in my book. They add different perspectives and make the journey more interesting.
